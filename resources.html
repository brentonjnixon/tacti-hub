M2 .50 Caliber Machine Gun PMCS

Critical pre-fire inspection steps to ensure proper headspace and timing adjustments before operation.

  • Verify barrel is fully screwed in and properly backed off the correct number of clicks(newer M2 .50 Cals no longer require manual H.S. & T.)
  • Check receiver assembly, barrel extension, and bolt for cracks or excessive wear.
  • Ensure proper lubrication on all moving parts using operational climate standards.

M240B Machine Gun PMCS

Rapid-scan operator maintenance checks for standard medium collective weapon systems.

  • Inspect the gas regulator plug and turn inside the gas collar to ensure seating
  • Check driving spring guide assembly for bends, kinks, or broken strands
  • Verify the feed tray, cover assembly, and feed paws move freely under spring tension.

M4A1 Carbine

Standard preventative maintenance checks and services for individual service rifles.

  • Inspect the bore and chamber for carbon buildup, pitting or obstructions.
  • Check gass rings on the bolt assembly to ensure they are staggered and holding compression.
  • Perform a standard function check to ensure safe, semi-automatice, and full auto settings engage correctly.
