Individual Tactical & Technical Tasks

Individual Tasks represent the foundational building blocks of unit combat readiness, focusing entirely on the technical and tactical proficiencies an individual Soldier must master. Unlike collective tasks that require synchronized team execution, these tasks evaluate a single Soldier's ability to operate equipment, communicate effectively, navigate terrain, and execute precision leadership procedures under simulated or live tactical conditions. Maintaining absolute mastery of these tasks ensures that every team member can function autonomously and seamlessly support the broader squad or platoon mission

The following individual tasks outline the baseline proficiency standards for small-unit combat arms leaders and Soldiers.

  • Troop Leading Procedures (TLPs)
  • Submit a SALUTE / SPOT Report
