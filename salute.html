SALUTE / SPOT Report Template

The SALUTE report is an established military reporting format used to transmit critical information regarding enemy activity, strength, and equipment. Use this digital template to draft precise, standardized intelligence reports before sending them up through communication channels.

Specify the number of enemy personnel, vehicles, or equipment observed.

Describe what the enemy is currently doing (traveling, digging in, establishing an OP).

Provide grid coordinates (MGRS), distance and direction from an unclassified objective, or landmarks.

Detail any patches, distinctive uniform items, camouflage patterns, or markings.

Enter the exact Date-Time Group (DTG) when the contact or observations occurred.

List any weapons, communications arrays, night vision devices, or specialized tools noticed.
