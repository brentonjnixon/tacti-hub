The Purpose of TACTiHUB

TACTiHUB was created to bridge the gap between traditional Army technical manuals and references to a more modern, mobile-first technology. In fast-paced operations operational environments, small-unit leaders need immediate access to critical documentation without digging through dense PDF files or hard copy manuals. This platform puts mission-essential guidance directly into the hands of the leaders who need it the most, and with this technology it has the power to develop and shape the future leaders and increase overall leathality.

Combat Arms Collective Focus

The core mission of TACTiHUB is supporting front-line small-unit leaders across multiple combat specialties. By breaking down complex tactics and maintenance procedures into rapid-scan checklists, leaders can quickly cross-reference standard operating procedures on the move. The platform integrates critical collective tasks such as Battle Drill 1A and Battle Drill 6 for dismounted operations alongside vehicle crew protocols and rapid PMCS guidance for heavy weapons and small arms.
