Troop Leading Procedures (TLP) Checklist

Troop Leading Procedures provide small-unit leaders with a reliable, structured framework to analyze a mission, develop a tactical plan, and prepare subordinates for execution. Use this interactive sequence to track planning milestones in the field.

1. Receive the Mission

Analyze the higher headquarters order using METT-TC variables to determine the unit's timeline, constraints, and specified tasks.

2. Issue a Warning Order (WARNORD)

Provide subordinates with initial instructions and essential timeline milestones immediately to maximize parallel planning time.

3. Make a Tentative Plan

Develop initial courses of action, conduct risk management assessments, and coordinate organic asset synchronization.

4. Initiate Movement

Direct the unit to begin repositioning, reconnaissance staging, or logistics logistics preparation if timeline constraints dictate.

5. Conduct Reconnaissance

Validate map assumptions and terrain features through physical observation, electronic systems, or scout assets whenever security permits.

6. Complete the Plan

Refine the initial course of action based on reconnaissance updates, and finalize graphic overlays, fires coordinates, and the layout of the order.

7. Issue the Order (OPORD)

Deliver the complete tactical operation order to subordinates, utilizing terrain models or graphic overlays to ensure maximum clarity and comprehension.

8. Supervise and Refine

Enforce standards through comprehensive pre-combat checks (PCCs), pre-combat inspections (PCIs), and full mission rehearsals to ensure operational execution matches the plan.
