Security Operations & Force Protection

Security operations are executed to provide early warning, destroy or displace enemy reconnaissance forces, and provide reaction time and maneuver space to the main body. These collective maneuvers require aggressive scouting, constant observation, and strict adherence to engagement criteria to preserve the main body's combat power.

Conduct a Screen Mission

Phase 1: Infiltration & Screen Line Establishment

The element maneuvers covertly to the designated screen line, establishing a series of overlapping observation posts. Main tracking efforts focus on identifying primary enemy avenues of approach and ensuring zero gaps exist along the security boundary.

Phase 2: Surveillance & Early Warning

Scout teams maintain continuous visual and electronic surveillance across the assigned sector. Early warning arrays detect enemy reconnaissance elements far in advance of the main body's position, establishing early tactical situational awareness.

Phase 3: Counter-Reconnaissance Engagement

Upon detecting enemy scouts, the element coordinates indirect fire assets or applies direct fire to destroy or repel threat reconnaissance forces. The goal is to defeat enemy tracking efforts without exposing the main defensive lines.

Phase 4: Displacement & Reporting

If pressed by an overwhelming enemy main body, the screening force executes a disciplined, rearward displacement along pre-planned alternate routes. The unit submits constant SALUTE reports to ensure the main commander retains maximum reaction time.

Establish & Maintain Local Security

Phase 1: Perimeter Orientation

Every small unit instantly establishes local security operations upon halting, regardless of the expected duration. Leaders designate specific firing sectors, establish a tight perimeter layout, and verify that all organic weapons systems possess proper interlocking fields of fire.

Phase 2: Stand-To Procedures

The unit enforces strict Stand-To protocols during high-probability danger periods, such as dawn and dusk. Personnel don full tactical gear, occupy primary fighting positions, and maintain absolute alert status to prevent enemy surprise attacks.

Phase 3: Listening Posts & Patrol Integration

Teams deploy short-range Listening Posts and Observation Posts (LP/OPs) forward of the main line during limited visibility. Continuous, overlapping security patrols clear immediate dead space and blind spots along the unit's perimeter.

Phase 4: Challenge & Password Enforcement

Sentries enforce strict challenge and password procedures using current operational codes to identify all personnel entering or exiting the sector. This process mitigates insider threats and denies unauthorized infiltration into the friendly area.
