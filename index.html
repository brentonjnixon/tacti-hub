What is TactiHub?

The Tactical Leadership Hub (TactiHub) is a mobile-ready technical knowledge base designed to modernize how small-unit combat arms leaders access mission-essential information. Specifically targeting Infantrymen (11B)ts for critical tasks like Battle Drills 1A and 6, alongside technical PMCS guidance for the M2, M240B, and M4 weapon systems.
