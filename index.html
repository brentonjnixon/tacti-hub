What is TactiHub?

The Tactical Leadership Hub (TactiHub) is a mobile-ready technical knowledge base designed to modernize how small-unit combat arms leaders access mission-essential information. Specifically targeting Military Occupation Specialties Infantrymen (11B), Cavalry Scouts (19D), Bradley Crewman (19C) for critical tasks like Battle Drills 1A and 6, alongside technical PMCS guidance for the M2, M240B, and M4 weapon systems.
