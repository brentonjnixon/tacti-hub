The Tactical Leadership Hub (Tacti-Hub) is a mobile-ready technical knowledge base designed to modernize how small-unit combat arms leaders access mission-essential information. Specifically targeting Infantrymen (11B), Cavalry Scouts (19D), and Bradley Crewmen (19C),
the platform replaces fragmented notes and dense regulatory manuals with streamlined, interactive checklists for critical tasks like
Battle Drills 1A and 6, alongside technical PMCS guidance for the M2, M240B, and M4 weapon systems.
